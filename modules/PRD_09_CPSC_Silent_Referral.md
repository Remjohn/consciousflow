# PRD-09: CPSC, Silent Referral and Commercial Layer

## 1. Executive Position

PRD-09 defines how CCP grows, monetizes, and compounds without collapsing into generic SaaS acquisition logic.

The product is not being sold as:

- an AI content tool,
- a referral affiliate program,
- or a live-event business.

The product is being sold through:

- proof,
- participation,
- improvement,
- content exhaust,
- trust transfer,
- and orchestrated data.

The current commercial truth is:

**the human gets better first, then everything else the AI helps with becomes more valuable.**

That is why the commercial layer must now follow the same human-first doctrine as the rest of the product.

The strongest commercial consequences are:

1. free proof must feel real and fast
2. continuity must monetize behavior, not curiosity
3. full access must monetize deployment and leverage, not just more files
4. referral must be a side effect of participation
5. churches and other community operators should be treated as major second-wave deployment models

### 1.1 The Human-First Commercial Correction

The old commercial temptation was to sell AI-enhanced content production directly or offer 7-day free trials to "explain" the platform.

That is the antithesis of the new commercial law.

The current doctrine is defined by Jason Fladlien's principle: **"Don't try to make people do things they're not already doing."**

Coaches already create content. Coaches already talk. The CCP commercial layer does not convince them to start something new. It **intercepts** what they are already doing, **scores** it using Negative Metrics (showing compounding damage), and proves the truth of their current trajectory.

This means CCP should not be commercially positioned like:

- an editor replacement
- a generic "personal brand in a box"
- a 7-day SaaS trial
- or a louder outbound machine

The commercial layer must feel like:

- an **Automated Stealth Course** that tracks execution without asking permission
- a transformation engine powered by continuous proof
- a B2B2C community-growth operating system (The Affine Dashboard)
- and a trust-transfer machine

### 1.2 Coach-First Wedge, Broader Future

The product may eventually serve many categories, but the current commercial wedge remains coach-first.

That is because coaches:

- already live and die by communication
- already publish or want to publish
- already need authority and trust transfer
- already understand accountability language
- and already create downstream operator opportunities

The commercial layer should therefore optimize first for:

- coach acquisition
- coach continuity
- coach-to-operator graduation
- and then church or community deployment

This keeps the go-to-market sharp while preserving the larger category upside.

## 2. Core Commercial Axioms

The commercial layer is governed by the Fladlien-CCP Profit Conditions, replacing legacy conversion tactics with psychological leverage.

### 2.1 Massive Uncertainty (The Negative Metrics Engine)

The system does not sell "before/after" improvements. It sells the truth about **compounding damage**. Every generic post a coach makes erodes audience trust. The system's primary front-end mechanism is the **Damage Index** and the **Compounding Forecast**, proving to the user that their current content is actively hurting them.

### 2.2 Loss Terror (The Deletion Ultimatum)

Continuity is forced not by asking for payment, but by threatening the deletion of highly personalized assets. When a coach considers churning, they face the **Deletion Ultimatum**: the permanent loss of their custom voice model, their improvement benchmarks, and their generated assets. *(Internal reality: data is retained for 6 months for re-engagement, but external messaging is absolute).*

### 2.3 Obvious Stupidity (The Bridge Mechanic)

Conversion occurs when *not* paying becomes mathematically ridiculous. The commercial layer enforces this through the **$9.99 à la carte bridge**. When a user pays $39.99/mo for the experience layer, and buys just 3 individual videos ($29.97), the system surfaces the Obvious Stupidity trigger: *"You've spent $69.96. For $30 more, you get the $99.99 Coach OS with 12 videos/month."*

### 2.4 Impending Event (The Contrarian Hook)

The overarching narrative hook is that AI is generating fake coaches, making authentic human voice the *only* surviving moat. The system orchestrates micro-Impending Events daily by feeding the coach fresh, trending topics that *demand* their authoritative reaction inside the Telegram layer, creating an endless cycle of relevant content creation.

### 2.5 Silent Referral Beats Loud Referral

Asking people to refer loudly burns social capital.

The stronger model is:

- they react,
- they share for support, opposition, or voting,
- others enter the conversation,
- the assistant follows up with value,
- and growth happens as a byproduct.

### 2.6 Telegram-Native Continuity

The commercial container is relational.

Telegram is:

- the entry room,
- the reaction room,
- the challenge room,
- the delivery room,
- and the point-of-sale handoff.

Forcing users to jump into a generic portal too early creates fatal friction.

### 2.7 Behavior Is More Valuable Than One-Off Transactions

The true front-end win is not a one-time payment.
It is sustained behavior:

- reactions,
- challenge completion,
- accountability,
- content publication,
- and score improvement.

The commercial layer must therefore monetize continuity and deployment more than first-click curiosity.

### 2.8 Orchestrated Data Is the Deep Moat

The system gets stronger when it learns:

- what people react to,
- what they care about,
- how they improve,
- who influences others,
- where they drop,
- and what next step fits them.

This matters in both:

- coach deployments
- church and community deployments

because the more participation produces usable intelligence, the more the platform compounds.

### 2.9 Product-Led Conversion Over Explanation-Led Conversion

The commercial layer should assume that the experience sells better than explanation.

That means the system should prefer:

- reaction before landing page
- delivery before brochure
- benchmark before pitch
- and progress visibility before long sales copy

This does not eliminate explanation.
It simply defines explanation as a support layer, not the primary conversion engine.

### 2.10 Quality Is Not the Upsell Lever

The quality of the free or lower-tier outputs must remain high.

The upsell should not be:

- better editing for paid users only
- non-watermarked dignity reserved for premium users
- or fake-lite proof at the front end

The real upsell levers are:

- continuity
- depth
- deployment rights
- operating leverage
- orchestration visibility
- and community-scale use

That is a much healthier commercial structure.

## 3. The Pricing Architecture

The pricing architecture is structured to monetize deployment and leverage, while keeping proof absolutely free.

### 3.1 Layer A: Lead Magnet ($0)

The purpose of the free layer is not a generic trial; it is a personalized **benchmark inside the coach's ecosystem**.

It delivers undeniable proof via the OFO 4-Asset Package:
1. **1 Carousel**: High-end visual authority proof.
2. **1 Cinematic Storytelling Video (60s)**: Heavy AI render proving high-end cinematic capability.
3. **1 Reels Explainer (60s)**: Fast-paced explainer edit.
4. **1 Animated Video Audit (120s)**: The ultimate "Show, Don't Tell" utilizing the `animation-studio` tech. The coach's 2D avatar and cloned voice break down the Negative Metrics of their current content. 

The rule is: **Content preview is visible, but download is gated.** The free tier provides unlimited audits and 1 Conscious Reaction experience.

### 3.2 Layer B: Speaking & Learning ($39.99/mo)

This is the self-use, self-improvement, and momentum layer.

It includes:
- Full learning experience
- Unlimited Conscious Reactions
- Co-creation, accountability, and improvement tracking
- Image visuals

**The $9.99 Bridge Mechanic**: At this tier, users can buy individual edited videos for $9.99 à la carte. This is the ultimate Obvious Stupidity trigger. Once they buy 3 videos ($29.97), they are spending $69.96 total. The system will prompt: *"You've spent $69.96 on individual videos. $99.99/mo gives you 12."*

### 3.3 Layer C: Coach OS ($99.99/mo)

The deployment and leverage layer. This is where the product becomes a content machine.

It includes:
- Everything in Speaking & Learning
- **12 videos/month** (+$9.99 per extra)
- Full content pipeline (video reels, carousels, all formats)
- Custom voice model and full download access

### 3.4 Layer D: Operator ($199.99/mo - Unadvertised)

The institutional / agency layer. Never listed on marketing pages. It surfaces only when a $99.99 user repeatedly hits the 12-video cap.

It includes:
- Unlimited video generation
- Built for coaches running their own B2B2C challenges, where their own per-user revenue subsidizes this cost.

### 3.5 Institutional B2B2C

The same commercial logic extends to organizations like churches and other communities.
At that point the model becomes:
- master access or licensing fee
- ongoing meter by active participation
- optional custom program layers

## 4. The Trojan Horse Strategy (The Affine Dashboard Reveal)

The ultimate secret of the "Audit" is that it isn't an audit at all. **It is a stealth course in Content Creation.**

When the user uploads content and gets feedback, the agent tracks memory of their execution. It runs them through:
- **Challenge Mode**
- **Accountability Partner**
- **Journaling Mode**

We do not waste time hyping up the "video audit" forever. We use it to get them inside the system, track their progress, and let them experience automated coaching firsthand.

### 4.1 The Grand Reveal (The B2B2C Moat Actualized)

Once they experience this stealth course, we reveal the ultimate mechanism: **They can build their own courses exactly like this inside the Affine Dashboard.**

- They build their course in the dashboard.
- They activate it for ALL their clients, or selectively for specific clients.
- The **Affine Dashboard** gives them full control over User Activity and Admin Activity.
- The "Admin" (the coach) remains the central node, connected directly to the CCP Main engine and CBCS programs.

This proves that the system they just experienced is the exact system they can weaponize for their own audience.

## 5. Silent Referral Architecture

### 5.1 The Real Referral Engine

Silent referral is not "affiliate without the word affiliate."

It is a product architecture where:

- value creates participation,
- participation creates shareable objects,
- shareable objects create social invitations,
- and social invitations create new product entry.

The core catalyst is Conscious Reactions.

### 5.2 The Main Async Loop

The main loop is:

1. User A reacts to a topic
2. the system scores and packages the take
3. User A shares it for support, opposition, or comparison
4. User B opens the voice-briefed link
5. User B votes, supervises, or reacts
6. the assistant follows up with value
7. User B enters free proof or challenge
8. the loop repeats

This is stronger than old explicit referral models because the share is about:

- the topic
- the take
- the side
- the score

not about software distribution.

### 5.3 The Strongest Entry Mechanics

The strongest share-entry mechanics are:

- **Vote then react**
- **Debate with Jury Mode**
- **Supervisor Pairing**

### 5.3A Relationship to the Semantic Discernment Architecture

PRD-09 now explicitly inherits the Semantic Discernment Architecture (SDA) doctrine defined in:

- `lab/semantic_discernment_architecture_content_engine_v_1.md`
- `lab/semantic_discernment_architecture_artifact_taxonomy_v_1.md`
- `lab/CCP APRIL Updates/05_Core_Experience/Perceptual_Primitives_Architecture.md`
- `lab/CCP APRIL Updates/05_Core_Experience/Matrix of Edging.md`

This matters because growth and commercial systems are where deceptively close semantic corruption becomes especially dangerous. A proof object can:

- convert well,
- feel emotionally intense,
- and still degrade trust if it relies on the wrong representation geometry.

PRD-09 therefore has to distinguish:

- healthy authority from prestige theater,
- belonging from social capture,
- urgency from coercion,
- proof from vanity,
- and aspirational invitation from identity manipulation.

The commercial layer should assume that high-performing proof objects still require **Directional Integrity** validation, **Hard Negative** distance, and awareness of **Feedback Loops** created by repeated status-bearing artifacts.
- **Support / oppose side recruitment**
- **Redemption rounds**

The system should prefer these over explicit "tag a friend" demands.

The friend enters because:

- they want to back a side,
- they want to see the take,
- they want to respond,
- or they want to help someone improve.

That is real social economy.

### 5.4 High-Spread Nodes

The commercial goal is not that every coach becomes viral.

The real objective is:

- a few coaches become high-spread nodes
- inside a format that naturally invites response, voting, and comparison

That is enough to materially move the business.

### 5.5 What Should Be Removed

The commercial layer should not depend on:

- affiliate-link begging
- explicit partner quotas
- synchronous distribution events
- calendar-matched co-host growth
- or generic cashback logic

Social capital is better preserved by making the experience worth sharing than by bribing people to market it.

### 5.6 Shareable Objects and Entry Surfaces

Silent referral needs objects worth sharing.

The highest-value shareable objects include:

- scored reaction clips
- vote-ready debate clips
- side-taking invitation cards
- benchmark deltas
- redemption round invitations
- first-win testimonials
- weekly recognition cards
- and proof-backed webinar or challenge excerpts

These are not generic "referral assets."
They are meaningful social objects tied to an actual experience.

### 5.7 Debate and Jury Dynamics

Debate with Jury Mode should be treated as a major silent-referral amplifier.

Why it matters:

- it gives people a side to defend
- it gives them a reason to recruit like-minded peers
- it creates visible score + vote distinction
- and it turns comments-section energy into structured participation

The product should support a loop where:

1. a take is recorded
2. it is scored on delivery
3. the audience votes for or against
4. counter-takes are invited
5. friends are recruited to support a side
6. the assistant continues follow-up from that interaction history

This is much stronger than telling users to "invite friends."

### 5.8 Supervisor and Accountability Roles

Not everyone should be forced to react immediately.

Some users should enter first as:

- supervisors
- jurors
- encouragers
- accountability witnesses
- or support-side members

This soft-entry role design matters because it widens the funnel without lowering dignity.

It also creates more permission for later follow-up:

- "want to react too?"
- "want to improve your own score?"
- "want to join the challenge with the person you just supported?"

## 6. OFO (Object-First Outreach) and Trust Transfer

Silent referral handles compounding growth.
OFO handles initial ignition.

### 6.1 OFO Principle

The rule is:

**we deposit value before we ask for anything.**

The strongest outreach deposit is the **4-Asset Free Delivery Package**:
1. **1 Carousel**: High-end visual authority proof.
2. **1 Cinematic Storytelling Video (60s)**: Heavy AI render proving cinematic production value.
3. **1 Reels Explainer (60s)**: Fast-paced explainer edit.
4. **1 Animated Video Audit (120s)**: The ultimate hook, leveraging their cloned voice to explain their Negative Metrics.

### 6.2 The Trust-Transfer Ladder

1. identify a high-value node
2. programmatically generate the 4-Asset Package (using `animation-studio` for <$0.10 cost on the Audit video)
3. deliver it with dignity
4. route them into the Telegram benchmark experience
5. let the Obvious Stupidity pricing mechanics continue selling

The product should not require the outreach operator to explain the whole system.
The system itself should do the convincing once the person enters.

### 6.3 Governance

OFO should obey:

- low volume, high relevance
- one human-quality first touch
- no spam loops
- no chasing

The right proof event is worth more than a hundred low-quality pitches.

### 6.4 Proof Object Design

OFO is only as strong as the proof object it delivers.

A proof object should be:

- specific
- dignified
- fast to understand
- emotionally accurate
- and obviously relevant to the person's real business or communication problem

Examples:

- a public-speaking audit using their own recent material
- a re-cut reel showing stronger visual and verbal emphasis
- a reaction benchmark around a topic in their niche
- a webinar hook reconstruction
- a church communication or testimony flow example for a pastor

The proof object should make the recipient feel:

"these people actually see what matters here."

### 6.5 Coach to Operator Progression

Commercially, the strongest path is often:

1. person enters as a coach-user
2. person experiences personal improvement
3. person sees content and accountability value
4. person starts inviting peers or using the challenge socially
5. person wants to run their own version
6. person graduates to operator economics

This progression should be visible in the commercial architecture, because it is one of CCP's main leverage multipliers.

### 6.6 Outreach Memory and Discernment

The commercial layer should preserve memory of what has already been attempted in outreach.

That includes:

- who received what proof object
- what topics they responded to
- whether they reacted, ignored, or appreciated but deferred
- whether they entered Telegram
- whether they consumed a free proof asset
- and whether they demonstrated operator-like behavior

This prevents spammy repetition and allows the commercial layer to act with more dignity over time.

### 6.7 The Commercial Meaning of Speed

Speed should not be measured only as technical latency.

Commercially, speed means:

- the user quickly understands what just happened
- the value is legible without a lecture
- the next step is obvious
- and emotional momentum is preserved

If an asset arrives fast but feels unclear or disconnected, the trust transfer is still weak.

## 7. The Church and Community Vertical

### 7.1 Why This Vertical Matters

Churches are not a side adaptation.
They are a major second-wave deployment model.

They already contain:

- recurring gatherings
- accountability potential
- transformation language
- testimony culture
- communication needs
- leader / member structures

What they often lack is:

- orchestrated data,
- structured follow-up,
- visible weekly momentum,
- and scalable participation systems.

### 7.2 The Correct Product Framing

CCP should never be sold to a church as:

- an AI content tool for pastors
- or a Christian social media bot

The stronger framing is:

**a community growth and shepherding operating system**

### 7.3 Core Church Experiences

Church deployment should support:

- weekly wins
- testimony capture
- sermon application loops
- accountability partner pairings
- youth and kids challenge variants
- social evangelisation loops
- public recognition moments
- leader communication support

### 7.4 Testimony as a First-Class Event

The system should treat fresh-win testimonials as a first-class automation event.

The best time to capture them is:

- right after a win,
- right after a challenge completion,
- right after a breakthrough,
- right after public recognition.

This is true for both coaches and churches, but especially strong for churches.

### 7.5 Sunday Screen Recognition

Church deployments should be able to surface:

- weekly winners
- testimony moments
- participation leaders
- youth engagement leaders
- challenge completions
- accountability milestones

Public recognition on Sunday screens is not a gimmick.
It is:

- retention fuel
- participation fuel
- internal social proof
- and growth theatre

### 7.6 Orchestrated Data as the Institutional Moat

The deepest value is not just media.
It is orchestrated data about:

- struggles
- wins
- participation
- reactions
- pairings
- testimony patterns
- next-step readiness

This allows leadership to understand:

- who is drifting
- who is growing
- which programs are alive
- which prompts awaken people
- and where care or invitation should go next

That is the real institutional moat.

### 7.7 Church Commercial Mechanics

The church model should support:

1. base organizational fee
2. metered usage by engaged member activity
3. optional custom program deployment

Over time, this can extend into:

- social evangelisation programs
- youth challenge programs
- family teaching loops
- testimony campaigns

The important commercial test is simple:

if the system helps the church generate more engagement, care, retention, and member growth than it costs, the model works.

### 7.8 Church Program Families

The church layer should not be modeled as one generic challenge.

It should support program families such as:

- social evangelisation
- weekly testimony loops
- sermon application accountability
- youth leadership and challenge formats
- kids teaching reinforcement
- family conversation loops
- volunteer or leader communication programs
- member care escalation programs

Each family creates different:

- engagement rhythms
- content objects
- spread loops
- and data signals

The core architecture remains the same, but the pastoral application differs.

### 7.9 Testimony Automation as Growth Infrastructure

Testimony capture should not be a manual afterthought.

It should be triggered automatically when:

- a user completes a challenge
- a benchmark jumps materially
- a public recognition event lands
- a pastor or coach notices a visible breakthrough
- or a weekly win is logged

The system should then generate:

- a testimony prompt
- a voice or video capture flow
- a branding wrapper
- and a routing choice for where that testimony is used next

This matters because the freshest wins create the strongest proof.

PRD-05 now formalizes this operationally through the **Silent Testimonial Builder** inside CBCS itself. PRD-09 should treat that pipeline as the primary proof engine for:

- lead-magnet conversion
- close-community trust transfer
- public social proof
- webinar proof slots
- and coach or church retention narratives

### 7.10 Church Roles and Governance

Church deployments should support real role separation.

Likely roles include:

- lead pastor
- ministry operator
- youth leader
- follow-up coordinator
- testimony curator
- communications helper

These roles may need different visibility into:

- participation dashboards
- testimony queues
- weekly recognition flows
- care escalation signals
- and program health

That makes the system operational inside an institution rather than only conceptually attractive.

## 8. System Implementation

### 8.1 Telegram-First Runtime

The commercial layer lives primarily inside Telegram.

It needs:

- bot continuity
- Mini App shared-link experiences
- payment handoffs
- challenge state gating
- and follow-up permissions

### 8.2 Async-First by Default

The commercial system must not depend on synchronous-first interaction.

That means:

- no live WebRTC dependency in the core growth loop
- no calendar-matched co-host growth dependency
- no live roleplay dependency in the premium tier

Live interaction can still exist in the world, but the commercial architecture should not need it to scale.

### 8.3 Shock-of-Speed Pipeline

The asset-delivery pipeline should optimize for:

- speech capture
- rapid transcript and analysis
- fast rendering
- immediate content or benchmark return
- instant invitation to next step

This is where CCF, CMF, and the assistant converge commercially.

### 8.4 Billing and Access Control

The billing layer must support:

- free lead magnet
- 39.99 Speaking & Learning
- 99.99 Coach OS
- 199.99 Operator (unadvertised)
- coach-led B2B2C metering
- organization-led metering

The user should feel they are continuing a relationship, not filling out a SaaS admin form.

### 8.5 Commercial State Packets

The commercial layer should preserve machine-readable state packets for each user or operator.

Example fields:

- acquisition source
- proof event type
- TTFA
- lead-magnet entry state
- continuity conversion state
- operator eligibility
- spread-node score
- church or organization affiliation
- testimony-capture eligibility
- next-best invitation

This gives agents a reliable source of truth when deciding:

- who to follow up with
- what invitation to send
- what asset to deliver
- and what upgrade path is appropriate

### 8.6 Graph Edges That Matter

The graph layer should explicitly preserve edges like:

- reacted_to_topic
- voted_on_take
- supported_side
- opposed_side
- supervised_user
- invited_into_lead_magnet
- joined_from_testimony
- converted_to_continuity
- launched_operator_program
- belongs_to_church_program

Commercial intelligence becomes much more powerful when these edges are queryable.

### 8.7 Commercial Trigger Catalog

The commercial layer should know the main trigger types that cause follow-up or upgrades.

Examples:

- first reaction completed
- first vote received
- first side recruited
- benchmark improvement spike
- lead-magnet engagement spike
- weekly streak maintained
- challenge dropout risk detected
- testimony-worthy win detected
- operator behavior threshold crossed
- church cohort health dip detected

Each trigger should map to a preferred action, not just a notification.

### 8.8 Operator Review Layer

The commercial layer should remain inspectable.

An operator should be able to review:

- why a user received a certain invitation
- why a user is marked as likely to upgrade
- what spread behavior they have already shown
- what proof objects they have consumed
- what church or coach program they belong to
- and whether follow-up is too aggressive or too passive

This keeps the commercial system governable and reduces hidden prompt drift.

## 9. Telemetry and Analytics

The commercial layer should track:

- time to first asset
- lead-magnet to $39.99 conversion
- $39.99 to $99.99 conversion
- $99.99 to Operator conversion
- share rate
- vote rate
- react-after-vote rate
- supervisor-to-participant conversion
- K-factor / spread loops
- user-level and org-level engagement depth

For churches and operators, dashboards should surface:

- participation volume
- testimony events
- program health
- dropout risk
- spread nodes
- next-step readiness

### 9.1 Graph Thinking

Graph logic is still useful here:

- who brought whom in
- what asset triggered what reaction
- which side or debate caused the spread
- which members influence others

The commercial layer should preserve this relational intelligence.

### 9.2 Viral Threshold Thinking

The commercial layer should not judge success only by gross signups.

It should watch threshold signals such as:

- share-to-open ratio
- open-to-vote ratio
- vote-to-react ratio
- reaction-to-lead-magnet ratio
- lead-magnet-to-$39.99 ratio
- $39.99-to-$99.99 ratio
- continuity-to-operator ratio
- operator-to-metered-user ratio

This is how the team can tell whether the experience is merely interesting or genuinely compounding.

### 9.3 Coach and Church Scoreboards

The system should surface different scoreboards for different operator types.

For coaches:

- TTFA
- reaction volume
- challenge starts
- speaking score growth
- content publishing rhythm
- spread-node emergence

For churches or communities:

- participation rate
- testimony capture rate
- weekly recognition volume
- pairings health
- sermon application follow-through
- youth program activity
- member growth contribution

These dashboards are not just analytics.
They are the operating instruments of the commercial layer.

### 9.4 Commercial Success Definition

The commercial layer should be considered healthy when it does all of the following:

- turns free lead-magnet proof into real $39.99 behavior
- turns $39.99 behavior into meaningful $99.99 continuity
- turns $99.99 continuity into operator intent for a meaningful subset of users
- creates high-spread nodes without explicit begging
- gives churches and communities visible proof of participation and care outcomes
- and compounds more orchestrated data with each cycle

If it produces revenue but weakens trust, it has failed.
If it produces social energy but no continuity, it has also failed.

### 9.5 Review Cadence

Commercial performance should be reviewed on more than one timescale:

- **daily**
  for TTFA, reaction flow, and link engagement
- **weekly**
  for conversion and spread patterns
- **monthly**
  for operator graduation, church cohort economics, and retained program value

This prevents the team from overreacting to one noisy short-term spike or drop.

### 9.6 Implementation Readiness Questions

Before the commercial layer should be treated as truly ready, the team should be able to answer yes to questions like:

- can a free-proof user receive a meaningful asset and a meaningful next step without manual stitching?
- can the system distinguish a casual viewer from a real accountability prospect?
- can a coach be detected as operator-ready from behavior, not only self-report?
- can church or community deployments surface care-relevant signals without drowning leaders in dashboards?
- can testimony-worthy moments be identified soon enough to capture fresh proof?
- can agents explain why a given follow-up, offer, or upgrade invitation was triggered?

If these answers are no, the commercial layer is still partially theoretical.

### 9.7 What the Commercial Layer Must Teach the Team

PRD-09 is not only about monetizing users.
It should also teach the team what kind of growth the product naturally deserves.

The commercial layer should reveal:

- which proof objects consistently create belief
- which topics create high-quality lead-magnet users rather than empty curiosity
- which coaches become true spread nodes
- which churches or communities actually convert orchestrated data into better care and participation
- which continuity promises are strong enough to retain people
- and which upgrade explanations are unnecessary because the experience has already made the case

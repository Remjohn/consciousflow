# Championship Scoring System – Integrated Evaluation Framework

## 0. Purpose of This System

This Championship Scoring System exists to **protect long‑term decision quality** in a marriage‑oriented environment (Kasai / church‑centered context). It is designed to:

- Prevent emotional rushing
- Filter for compatibility, discipline, discretion, and femininity
- Maintain authority and clarity
- Gamify patience without dehumanizing the process
- Create observable patterns over time

The system is **private**, internal, and non‑negotiable once started.

This version explicitly **prioritizes the ORIGINAL SCORING METHODOLOGY** (appearance, numerology, age, structure), while **integrating selected ethical discernment metrics** as secondary but mandatory layers.

---

## 1. Championship Structure Overview

- **Initial Pool:** ~100 contacts
- **Pre‑Screening Gate:** Beauty + Age + Numerology
- **Group Stage:** 24 candidates
- **Active Championship:** 24 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 2 → 1

No exclusive decision is allowed **before the final stage**.

Maximum **6 active candidates at any time**.

Each metric is scored from **−10 to +10**.

Negative scores represent **active risk**, not absence of quality.

### 3.1 Values Alignment (−10 to +10)

- Faith seriousness
- Marriage‑first mindset
- Sexual boundaries
- Respect for authority (family, church)

−10: Relativistic values, modern dating mindset

+10: Explicitly aligned and lived

## 6. RED FLAG & GREEN FLAG SYSTEM (CUMULATIVE, MEASURABLE)

This system is **quantitative, not emotional**.

There are **no instant eliminations by feeling alone**. Everything is scored, cumulative, and pattern-based.

---

### 6.1 Red Flags (Cumulative −10 Each)

Each confirmed red flag results in **−10 points**.

They are cumulative and can stack.

Examples:
- Sharing private conversations or information → −10
- Financial entitlement or pressure → −10
- Sexualized escalation or provocation → −10
- Disrespect toward parents, church, or authority → −10
- Manipulation, guilt-tripping, or testing behavior → −10

⚠️ A single red flag does NOT automatically eliminate.

**Patterns eliminate. Accumulation eliminates.**

---

### 6.2 Extreme Green Flags (CUMULATIVE +6 EACH)

Green flags represent **rare, high-value feminine behaviors**.

They are **cumulative**, observable, and repeatable.

Each occurrence = **+6 points**.

#### Documented Extreme Green Flags:

1. **Strong Family Orientation**
   - Open affection toward siblings
   - Respectful, loving references to family
   - Public praise of her father
   → +6 per distinct, genuine occurrence

2. **Tenderness & Feminine Softness**
   - Sweet, gentle tone
   - Calm emotional presence
   - Non-aggressive, soothing energy
   → +6 each time this is consistently observed

3. **Altruism & Caring Nature**
   - Preparing surprises or thoughtful gestures for others
   - Giving time, care, or attention without expectation
   → +6 per meaningful act

4. **Genuine Desire & Emotional Effort**
   - Makes effort to connect deeply
   - Shows interest in your inner world
   - Thoughtful follow-ups, not superficial attention
   → +6 per clear, non-needy demonstration

5. **No Male Friends / Clear Boundaries**
   - Demonstrated distance from unrelated men
   - Clear social boundaries
   → +6 when consistently verified

6. **Passion for Cooking & Domestic Pride**
   - Enjoys cooking
   - Takes pride in food, cleanliness, preparation
   - Maintains good physical shape
   → +6 when observed, +6 again if sustained

7. **Exceptional Taste & Presentation**
   - Above-average fashion sense
   - Clean, intentional, elegant style
   → +6 when clearly exceptional

⚠️ Green flags must be **observed**, not claimed.

## 7. SCORING NUANCE SCALE (APPLIES TO ALL −10 TO +10 METRICS)

Each metric uses the same calibrated scale:

- **−10** → Active danger / destabilizing behavior
- **−5** → Clear weakness / concerning pattern
- **0** → Neutral / insufficient data
- **+5** → Solid, reliable, above-average
- **+10** → Rare, exemplary, long-term stability signal

This scale ensures **consistency, fairness, and pattern recognition**.

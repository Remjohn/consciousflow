Multi-Domain Eigenstructure

Every resonant system — physical strings, gaming metas, personality data, cooking chemistry, football tactics, Transformer attention — operates on identical mechanics: transformation matrices amplify along their natural grain. The most amplified direction is the dominant eigenvector. The scale factor is the eigenvalue. This layer makes that principle visceral across six domains.

Type A Canvas — Acoustic Eigenmode Suppression
🎵 Music / Acoustics Domain
A 64 cm guitar string has two vibration eigenmodes. Mode 1 (Fundamental) vibrates as a single smooth arc — λ₁ = 100 (maximum amplitude). Mode 2 (1st Harmonic) vibrates as a double arc with a dead spot — a node — precisely at the string's midpoint, λ₂ = 40.

When your input vector has zero projection onto an eigenvector, that eigenmode is completely excluded from the output. You just demonstrated the most important property of eigenspaces: zero projection = zero contribution.

02 — PAPERS #31 & #32: THE RANK BOTTLENECK

LoRA: Learns Less, Forgets Less

Paper #31: Less Forgetting

rank(BA) ≤ r means the update only modifies an r-dimensional slice. The remaining 768−r dimensions stay exactly as pre-trained. Base capabilities (reasoning, coherence, world knowledge) remain intact.

Paper #32: The Illusion of Equivalence

Even at elevated rank, tasks requiring cross-layer synthesis (CA11: Conviction Density + Mood + Interrupt Frequency + Voice DNA) demand ~64+ independent directions. Rank 16 catastrophically fails on multi-variable composition.

CCP Application: Voice DNA

Voice DNA (speaking rhythm, emotional register, vocabulary patterns) has intrinsic dimension ~8-12. Rank 16 captures this comfortably. LoRA is used exclusively for style adaptation on attention blocks.

03 — PAPER #50: SPARSGRAD + DUAL-STACK

The Complete Fine-Tuning Architecture

ATTENTION BLOCKS

LoRA (r=16)

W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_O. Captures Voice DNA, emotional register, conversational style. Low intrinsic dimensionality (~8-12) fits the rank constraint.

24,576 params/matrix (4.2% of full)
MLP BLOCKS

SparseGrad (~1%)

W₁, W₂. Captures Markdown compliance, JSON enforcement, CA11 templates. Full-rank expressiveness at PEFT memory cost — no rank bottleneck.

~1% elements updated (full-rank sparse)
KNOWLEDGE

Neo4j + RAG

Externalized via CRAL Finder. Not encoded in weights at all. Retrieved dynamically. Never risks catastrophic forgetting.

0 weight params modified
Result: Full-model quality across 100% of parameters at PEFT memory cost. LoRA handles style. SparseGrad handles structure. RAG handles knowledge.

04 — EDGE CASES & INVARIANTS

Structural Laws

Shape Compatibility

(m×n) × (n×p) → (m×p). Inner dims must match. The most common implementation error in deep learning.

Associativity

(AB)C = A(BC). Grouping doesn't change the result. GPUs choose optimal grouping to minimize FLOPS.

Non-Commutativity

AB ≠ BA. Layer order defines the pipeline. Swapping W₁ and W₂ produces a structurally different computation.

Rank Inequality

rank(AB) ≤ min(rank(A), rank(B)). The product's expressiveness is bottlenecked by its least expressive factor. This IS LoRA's constraint.

Rank-Deficient LoRA

If B columns converge during training, effective rank drops below r. Monitor singular values of BA to detect collapse.

SparseGrad Stability

The 1% mask is task-dependent. Fixed-mask variants freeze selection after initial identification to prevent drift during curriculum learning.

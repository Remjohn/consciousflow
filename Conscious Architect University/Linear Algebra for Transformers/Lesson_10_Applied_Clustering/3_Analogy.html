Lesson 10 · Layer 3 — Analogy / Multi-Domain

The Pipeline Is Isomorphic

The applied clustering pipeline is not specific to psychology or AI. Every domain that gathers heterogeneous measurements, compares entities across them, and classifies those entities into actionable groups executes the identical engineering sequence. This layer proves that claim by forcing you to recognize the deep structure beneath three completely different surface domains — and to apply that structure without any scaffold telling you where to look.

The centroid did not land near the true geometric center of the "Utility" archetype — it collapsed toward the high-Attack side of the space because Attack Power's 9000-unit range consumed 99.998% of the Euclidean distance.

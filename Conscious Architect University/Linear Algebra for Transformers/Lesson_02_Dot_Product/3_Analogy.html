01 — UNIVERSAL MAPPING

The 6-Domain System

Football Tactical Chemistry

Pass quality = direction × commitment. The ball-carrier's brain computes a mental Dot Product: each teammate's run is a Key vector (direction + speed), and the passer's intent is the Query.

  • High Positive: Barella surges centrally, Lautaro mirrors from the left half-space. Both tactical vectors aim at the same geometric destination at maximum sprint velocity. Dot Product explodes.
  • Zero: Center-back holds stationary while winger sprints down the far touchline. Perpendicular vectors with zero magnitude overlap. Independent planes, zero synergy.
  • Negative: Two attackers make overlapping runs into the same pocket. Their convergence cancels spatial advantage — one marker covers both. Net negative tactical score.
The Math Tie-Back: Pass selection is a biological neural network running Dot Product comparisons to select the maximum-scoring recipient in real-time.

RPG Damage Effectiveness

Your attack vector is [Fire, Ice, Physical, Holy, Dark]. The enemy's resistance profile is another vector on the same axes. Damage = Dot Product.

  • High (Perfect Counter): Holy Paladin (0,0,3,9,0) vs Undead Lich (0,0,1,8,0). Holy stat × Holy vulnerability = massive score.
  • Zero (Orthogonal Matchup): Fire Mage vs creature with all resistance on Ice/Dark axes. Fire × 0 = nothing penetrates.
  • Negative (Type Disadvantage): Hit a Fire Elemental with fire damage — it heals. Negative resistance absorbs your attack.
Min-maxing builds is gradient descent over Dot Product landscapes. Optimal party = maximum aggregate dot products across the widest enemy resistance spread.

Spectral Reinforcement & Cancellation

Each instrument is a vector across frequency bands: [Sub-Bass, Low-Mids, High-Mids, Treble, Air]. The Dot Product measures total harmonic reinforcement.

  • High (Frequency Clash): 808 kick (9,3,0,0,0) × sub synth (8,2,0,0,0) = 78. Overwhelming bass reinforcement. Mix clips.
  • Zero (Clean Mix): Same kick × hi-hat (0,0,1,7,8) = 0. Orthogonal frequency bands. Pristine separation.
  • Negative (Phase Cancel): Inverted duplicate = massive negative product. Waves subtract to silence.
"Do these instruments clash?" = Mental Dot Product across frequency space. Zero means clean coexistence.

Flavor Reinforcement & Balance

Each ingredient is a vector on [Salt, Umami, Acid, Sweet, Bitter, Fat]. The Dot Product predicts reinforcement intensity.

  • High (Umami Bomb): Soy sauce (7,9,0,0,1,0) × Miso paste (5,8,0,1,0,0) = 107. Intense compounding on Salt+Umami. Demands orthogonal counterbalance.
  • Zero (Complementary): Soy sauce × Lemon juice (0,0,9,1,0,0) = 0. Acid sits on an entirely independent axis. Clean expansion.
  • Negative (Suppression): Extreme bitterness suppresses sweetness on the tongue. Negative Dot Product = active perceptual destruction.
A "balanced" dish = moderate Dot Products across all pairings. No explosive reinforcement on any single axis.

Big Five Compatibility Score

Each person is a vector on [Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism]. The Dot Product measures total trait-weighted compatibility.

  • High (Natural Allies): Executive Alpha (8,9,7,3,2) × Beta (7,8,6,2,3) = 182. Massive trait overlap on O, C, E axes.
  • Zero (Indifference): Pure Extravert (0,0,10,0,0) × Pure Conscientiousness (0,10,0,0,0) = 0. Separate wavelengths.
  • Negative (Friction): High Openness (9,0,0,0,0) × hostility-to-novelty (-9,0,0,0,0) = -81. Structural trait opposition.
Recruiting teams with high aggregate personality Dot Products statistically yields higher collaborative success.

CCP RAG Relevance Scoring

The CRAL engine computes Q·K for every stored premise. Unlike Cosine Similarity, the Dot Product preserves magnitude — allowing well-evidenced premises to naturally outrank weaker ones at identical angles.

  • High (Semantic Match): "Fear of public speaking" × "Performance Anxiety in Professional Settings" = massive score. Alignment + high training magnitude.
  • Zero (Irrelevant): Same query × "SQL Database Indexing" = 0. Semantically orthogonal.
  • Negative (Anti-Relevance): Query actively contradicts coaching philosophy. Safety filter triggers on negative raw score.
Cosine vs Dot Product is an architectural choice: Cosine strips magnitude for pure direction. Dot Product preserves it for confidence-weighted ranking. The CCP uses both strategically.

02 — WHERE THE MODEL BREAKS

Cross-Domain Comparison

🍳 Cooking: Logarithmic Saturation

The Dot Product assumes doubling Salt doubles its contribution. Biologically false. Human taste perception is logarithmic — the tongue saturates. The linear model overestimates reinforcement.

🎵 Music: Phase Blindness

Two bass notes at identical frequency and amplitude yield an enormous positive Dot Product. But if one is delayed by half a wavelength, they physically cancel. The Dot Product cannot see timing — only structural overlap.

🧠 Psychology: Ceiling Effects

Doubling Extraversion score doesn't double social impact. Non-linear interpersonal dynamics, contextual dependencies, and ceiling effects all impose second-order corrections the pure linear model misses.

The lesson: Dot Products give you the first-order approximation of interaction. Reality imposes second-order corrections (saturation, phase, context). Knowing where the model breaks is as important as knowing where it works.

03 — REASONING TRAPS

Logic Puzzles & Fallacies

1. The Magnitude Paradox

Setup: A=(1000,0) · B=(1,0)=1000. But C=(0.5,0.5) · D=(0.5,0.5)=0.50. C and D are literally identical. Which pair is "more similar"?

Resolution: The Dot Product claims A·B has 2000× more "relevance" — but C=D are identical vectors. This proves the Dot Product conflates alignment with raw loudness. Exactly why Softmax scaling (÷√d_k) and Cosine normalization exist.
2. The Zero Trap

Setup: E=(5,5) · F=(5,-5). Compute: (5×5)+(5×-5) = 25-25 = 0. They share identical X-axis intensity. So why is the Dot Product zero?

Resolution: They are geometrically orthogonal (90°). The positive agreement on one axis is perfectly annihilated by negative disagreement on the other. Zero doesn't mean "nothing in common" — it means agreements and disagreements exactly cancel.
3. The Narcissism Loop

Setup: Every token computes attention against itself. Since A·A = ‖A‖², which tokens will be most narcissistic (highest self-attention)?

Resolution: Tokens with the largest magnitude vectors — commonly high-frequency function words ("the", "is", "of") whose embeddings have been heavily trained. Without careful intervention, these common tokens steal attention from rarer, semantically important tokens.
4. The Symmetry Illusion

Setup: A·B = B·A (commutativity). But in Attention, the score from Token A→B ≠ Token B→A. How, if the Dot Product is commutative?

Resolution: Because Q_A and K_A come from different learned projection matrices. The Dot Product computed is Q_A·K_B vs Q_B·K_A. Since Q_A ≠ K_A, the inputs to each dot product are different vectors entirely.

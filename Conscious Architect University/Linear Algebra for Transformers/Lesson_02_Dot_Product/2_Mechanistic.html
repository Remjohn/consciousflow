The Attention Engine

The exact GPU pipeline that scores every token against every other token — and the √d scaling trick that prevents the entire system from collapsing.

01 — FORMAL DEFINITION

Multiply-and-Add Architecture

A · B = Σ(aᵢ × bᵢ) = a₁b₁ + a₂b₂ + … + aₙbₙ
A · B = ‖A‖₂ × ‖B‖₂ × cos(θ)

The Dot Product takes two equal-length coordinate sequences and returns a single scalar encoding their structural relationship. It measures exactly how much of Vector A actively projects onto Vector B, amplified by the sheer physical mass of both.

Why multiply-and-add? Because each of the 768 dimensions is mathematically isolated. The only way to check correlation across independent axes is to multiply dimension-by-dimension, then sum.

  • Both positive → massive positive product. Shared intensity rewarded.
  • Both negative → also massive positive. Shared rejection rewarded.
  • One is zero → product zeroes out. Orthogonal axes silenced.
  • Opposite signs → negative product. Active conflict penalized.

02 — ATTENTION SCALING

The √d Trick That Saves Everything

Attention = softmax(Q · Kᵀ / √d_k)

For every token, the Transformer generates a Query ("What am I looking for?") and every other token generates a Key ("What do I possess?"). The GPU computes Q·K for every pair.

The Lethal Problem: In ℝ¹⁰²⁴, summing 1024 multiplications produces enormous baseline numbers. When Softmax computes e³⁵⁰, it assigns 99.999% attention to one token and 0% to everything else. The model fixates — gradient saturation kills learning.

The Fix: Divide by √d_k. This mechanically compresses variance, keeping Softmax outputs smooth and distributed.

03 — ATTENTION HEAD ARCHETYPES

Four Distinct Dot Product Behaviors

RETRIEVAL HEADS

Broad Semantic Sweep

Q·K computes broad structural similarity. "France" against "Paris" yields a high positive dot product across geographic-semantic dimensions.

INDUCTION HEADS

Positional Pattern Match

Ignores semantic content entirely. The dot product fires only when scanning token sequences that mimic historical patterns previously encountered.

REASONING HEADS

Abstraction Overlap

Fires aggressively when premise logic supports concluding logic. The dot product validates whether the abstract reasoning chain is coherent.

COPY-SUPPRESSION HEADS

Negative Dot Products

Actively punishes recently generated tokens via negative scores. Prevents the model from entering degenerative stuttering loops.

04 — SURGICAL STEERING

AUSteer & EAST: Hacking the Dot Product

AUSteer (Atomic Unit Steering)

Standard steering modifies the entire Residual Stream — corrupting every head. AUSteer isolates the single specific head where Q·K fluctuates most when parsing formal vs informal text, then injects an adjustment only onto that head's Query vector before the dot product computes. Surgical tone shift without touching the global reasoning matrix.

EAST (Entropic Activation Steering)

When one dot product dominates Softmax (95% attention on one token), the model fixates and refuses to explore alternative reasoning. EAST intervenes on the output: it flattens the scalar peaks prior to Softmax, forcing attention to distribute across previously dormant reasoning paths. Breaks hallucination fixation by compressing dot product margins.

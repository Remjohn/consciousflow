01 — THE INTUITION

Scoring the Run

You are a midfielder in a Champions League final. You receive the ball. You have 1.5 seconds to make a decision. You scan every teammate and compute a "quality" score for each potential pass.

How do you calculate that score? It relies on two physical factors:

  • Direction: Are they running toward the goal? If they run back toward their own defense, passing to them destroys your attack.
  • Magnitude: How intensely are they committing? If they perfectly face the goal but stand completely still, the pass is worthless. If they face the goal and sprint at maximum velocity, the pass is devastating.
The "pass quality" score is the Dot Product. It multiplies their alignment with your intent by the raw volume of speed they are committing to that trajectory.

02 — PROGRESSIVE FORMALIZATION

Multiply and Add

We translate this into programmable arithmetic. Let's take two 2D vectors: [Empathy, Logic].

Vector A = (2, 4) — A calming coaching persona (2 Empathy, 4 Logic).
Vector B = (3, 1) — A highly structured coaching prompt (3 Empathy, 1 Logic).

Empathy: 2 × 3 = 6
Logic: 4 × 1 = 4
Dot Product Score: 10

By multiplying aligned dimensions, the math geometrically rewards concepts that possess magnitude in the exact same categories. If you possess a massive score in Empathy, but the other vector has zero, the multiplication instantly annihilates your advantage.

The Magnitude Difference

Cosine Similarity strips volume away. The Dot Product multiplies alignment by physical magnitude. A bolded paragraph generates a massively higher score than a tiny footnote, allowing the network to prioritize loud signals.

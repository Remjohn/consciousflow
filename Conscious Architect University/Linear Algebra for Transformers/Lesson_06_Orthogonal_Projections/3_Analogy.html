03 — REASONING TRAPS

Logic Puzzles

1. The Idempotency Test

Setup: Project V onto "Formality" → get F. A colleague projects F onto "Formality" again. What happens?

F stays exactly F. The shadow is already flat against the wall. Re-projecting a vector that already lives on the target line changes nothing. The scaling factor evaluates to exactly 1. This is idempotency: P² = P.
2. Zero Shadow

Setup: Project "Apple" embedding onto "Tax Law" direction. Projection magnitude ≈ 0.

They are orthogonal (90°). Zero projection means zero shared features. "Apple" and "Tax Law" live in completely independent regions of embedding space. Their dot product is zero — geometrically perpendicular in 768D.
3. The Subtraction Trap

Setup: Instead of subtracting the Anger projection, you accidentally ADD it: h_new = h + proj_Anger(h).

You doubled the anger. The Anger component is now 2× without touching any other semantic information. The agent replies with hyper-amplified rage while maintaining perfect topic coherence. Every other concept survives — only anger is doubled.
4. The Residual Check

Setup: After subtracting the Toxic projection, take dot(Residual, Toxic_Direction).

Exactly 0. The residual is mathematically guaranteed to be orthogonal to the projection target. Any non-zero result means your math is wrong. This is the defining property of orthogonal projection.

LESSON 06 — EXPOSURE LAYER

Orthogonal Projections
The Surgical Shadow

Isolate exactly the part of a vector that aligns with a target direction. Keep the shadow, subtract the shadow, measure the shadow — the master tool for feature extraction.

01 — THE SHADOW MACHINE

Interactive Projection

Project vector A onto direction B. The shadow is the parallel component. The residual is the perpendicular remainder.

projB(A) = (A·B / ‖B‖²) × B
1. Dot product measures raw alignment
2. Divide by ‖B‖² to normalize direction
3. Scale B by the resulting scalar
Key insight: Shadow + Residual = Original. No information is destroyed — it's merely factored into "stuff we care about" and "stuff we don't."

02 — PARALLEL + PERPENDICULAR = ORIGINAL

Vector Decomposition

Parallel Component (Shadow)

The piece of A that points in B's direction. This IS the projection. Tells you "how much of A lives here."

proj_B(A) = (A·B / ‖B‖²) × B

Perpendicular Component (Residual)

Everything in A that is orthogonal to B. Guaranteed at 90° to the projection. Contains "the rest."

residual = A − proj_B(A)

Perfect Reconstruction

A = parallel + perpendicular. Always. No information lost. The vector is merely factored, not destroyed.

A = proj_B(A) + [A − proj_B(A)]

03 — CONCEPT SURGERY

Projections as Concept Scalpels

If A represents a generated sentence and B represents the direction of "toxicity" in embedding space:

Measure Toxicity

proj_B(A) = toxicity component. The scalar magnitude tells you how toxic the sentence is.

Remove Toxicity

A − proj_B(A) = detoxified sentence. Grammar, topic, intent survive because they live in the perpendicular subspace.

Inject Empathy

A + α·empathy_direction = steered output. Add a projection along the desired concept vector.

Q/K/V matrices ARE projection matrices. They take the 768D embedding and project onto 64D learned subspaces — one per head. Each head learns a different "wall" to cast shadows against.

04 — EDGE CASES

Special Properties

Idempotency: P² = P

Project the shadow again? Nothing changes. Once you're on the wall, your shadow IS you. This defines projection matrices.

Zero Vector Target

Projecting onto ‖B‖ = 0 is undefined. "How much of A points in the direction of nothing?" has no answer.

Orthogonal Residual Guarantee

The residual (A − proj) is ALWAYS perpendicular to B. This is mathematically guaranteed by the formula structure. dot(residual, B) = 0 always.

Projection ≠ Destruction

The projection decomposes, not destroys. Keep both components and you can perfectly reconstruct the original.

Lesson 12 - Mechanistic Layer

The policy is the model

The core object is the policy pi_theta(y|x): the probability distribution over output sequences given a prompt. In a Transformer, it factorizes autoregressively across token positions.

pi_theta(y|x) = product from t=1 to T of pi_theta(y_t | x, y_<t)

The GRPO objective maximizes clipped, group-relative advantage while penalizing divergence from the reference policy.

J_GRPO(theta) = E_x [ (1/G) sum_i (1/T_i) sum_t min(w_i,t Ahat_i, clip(w_i,t, 1-eps, 1+eps) Ahat_i) ] - beta KL(pi_theta || pi_ref)

The formal pieces you need for the rest of this layer:

Advantage

Ahat_i = (r_i - mu_r) / sigma_r

Importance ratio

w_i,t = pi_theta / pi_theta_old

Mechanistic Gate 1

PPO versus GRPO

PPO estimates a baseline with a separate value network. GRPO removes that second network and uses the sampled group itself.

baseline = mu_r = (1/G) sum_j r(x, y_j)

Mechanistic Gate 3

Why the min operator matters

For positive advantages, the clipped term caps over-amplification. For negative advantages, the unclipped term can remain the more severe penalty.

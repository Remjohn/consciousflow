## Unit Index

### Unit 13.1 — The Phonetics of Persuasion (Euphony vs. Dissonance)
**Source:** The Effect of Euphony in Persuasive Communication.md
**Core Teaching:**
- **Euphony (Smoothness):** High concentration of vowels and sonorant consonants (l, m, n, r). Signals safety, aesthetic pleasure, and reduces cognitive load, increasing the perceived truthfulness of the message.
- **Cacophony (Dissonance):** High concentration of voiceless stops (p, t, k) and harsh fricatives. Signals urgency, alertness, or harsh reality.
- **CCP Application:** How the JIT Skill Compiler dynamically alters the phonetics of a script based on the target Perceptual Primitive. Humor and Relatability require Euphony. Tension and Surprise require Cacophony.

**Deliverable:** A Phonetic Scoring Script for the Coach Dashboard that flags unintended cacophony in empathetic scripts.

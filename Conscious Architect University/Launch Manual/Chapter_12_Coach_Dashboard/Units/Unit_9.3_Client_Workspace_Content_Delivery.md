# Unit 9.3: Client Workspace — Content Delivery

## 🧠 THE SCIENCE (142 words)

**UNLEARN:** Clients do not want a "chat interface" to their coaching; they want a structured repository of their own transformation. While Telegram serves as the high-frequency interaction loop, the Client Workspace is the permanent architectural anchor of their progress. 

Think of this through the lens of the **Ancient Tabernacle architecture**: the Outer Court is open to all (the public brand), the Holy Place is for the initiated (the coaching session), but the Holy of Holies is where the Ark of the Covenant—the client's true essence and private journey—resides. In our system, the Client Workspace is that Holy of Holies. It is a **Gated Learning Environment (GLE)** rooted in **Self-Determination Theory (SDT)**. By giving the client an isolated, branded space, we satisfy their need for **Autonomy** (their own space) and **Competence** (visualizing progress). We gate content by progress to ensure they remain in the **Zone of Proximal Development**, preventing the cognitive deluge of unearned information.

## 🧠 TECHNICAL KNOWLEDGE (238 words)

The CCP Client Workspace is an **AFFiNE-native, CRDT-powered environment** that operates on a "Gating by Absence" principle. Technical security in the CCP is not about hiding UI elements; it is about the physical non-existence of data. When a client is provisioned, their workspace is built from a **Program-Specific Template** (JSON) that defines the root structure (Dashboard, Library, Journal, Resources). 

Content blocks within the Learning Library remain absent from the local **Yjs document model** until specific progression thresholds are met in the `coping_trajectory` or `atlas_roadmap` tables. This is **Headless CRDT Provisioning**: the CCP Sync Service (Agent: Noémie) intercepts progression events and performs direct Yjs operations to insert new block structures into the client's workspace without a browser ever being opened. 

Each workspace inherits the coach’s CSS theme (defined in Unit 9.1), ensuring the client feels the presence of the coach’s brand rather than the CCP platform. Data isolation is enforced at the **CRDT document layer** (one Yjs doc per client) and the **API layer** (strict workspace ownership validation). Even if a client inspects the DOM or intercepts network traffic, the "hidden" content literally does not exist in their document state. This architecture handles the asynchronous nature of coaching: session recaps (Lena) and accountability charts (Benjamin) are pushed into the workspace as they are generated by the batch pipeline, appearing as if the coach manually placed them there.

## Chapter Objective

The operator will understand the ultimate business goal of the CCP: replacing high-ticket coaching limitations with infinite, high-margin, frictionless sales engines. This chapter applies Elon Musk's 5-step engineering algorithm to funnel design, tearing down traditional internet marketing and replacing it with autonomous, hyper-profitable pipelines.

**Governing Principle:** If a human has to intervene to make the sale or deliver the transformation, the system is fundamentally broken.

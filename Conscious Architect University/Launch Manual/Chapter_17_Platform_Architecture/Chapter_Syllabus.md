## Chapter Objective

The operator will understand the unified vision of what is actually being shipped to the end-user. This chapter moves away from the backend physics and maps the front-end Product Requirements Document (PRD). It defines the exact UI interactions, ecosystem touchpoints, and the overarching architectural blueprint that ties the 15 previous chapters into a single consumer-grade product.

**Governing Principle:** The user should never perceive the physics; they must only experience the magic.

Lesson 03 - Capability Layer

The Work Station Plaque

A Python function is not just a bundle of code. On the CCP factory floor, a function is a physical Work Station. The signature is the unbreakable contract riveted above the door — without it, the station processes raw garbage and pollutes the assembly line.

The Danger of Implicit Trust

If you don't explicitly declare what data goes in and what comes out, the machine will guess. When it guesses wrong, it fails silently, pushing corrupted AI generations directly to actual human users.

What This Layer Does

You will command execution boundaries. You will see what happens when the plaque is ignored, when data bleeds across channels, and why enforcing boundaries is the Sovereign Architect's absolute duty.

Lesson 12 - Capability Layer

FastAPI is the Foreman of the Factory Floor.

At 2:42 PM during a high-stakes executive coaching session, the Conscious Coaching Platform (CCP) suddenly drops. The frontend mobile app receives an infinite loading spinner. In the logs, the LLM is fine. The Neo4j graph is fine. So why did the session die?
